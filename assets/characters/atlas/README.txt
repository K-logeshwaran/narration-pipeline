ATLAS BULB MASCOT KIT
All layers are 1000x1000 px transparent PNGs. Stack them at (0,0), no offsets:
  1. base.png                (bulb, rays, base, faint globe texture)
  2. eyes_open.png  OR  eyes_closed.png   (blink every 3-5 seconds, about 0.12 s closed)
  3. mouth_<letter>.png      (Rhubarb mouth shapes)
Rhubarb letters: X idle/silence, A closed (P B M), B slightly open (K S T),
C open (EH), D wide open (AA), E rounded (AO ER), F puckered (UW OW W),
G teeth on lip (F V), H tongue up (L).
Idle motion ideas: slow vertical bob of about 6 px, tiny squash on loud words.
